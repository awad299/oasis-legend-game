# Creative Commons Zero game assets

The rigged animated character meshes shipped here were authored by Kay Lousberg (KayKit Adventurers, CC0 1.0) and distributed as optimized game-ready GLBs by sion-rgb in tactical-slash under THIRD_PARTY_ASSETS.md.

- Original character pack: https://github.com/KayKit-Game-Assets/KayKit-Character-Pack-Adventures-1.0
- Selected optimized character models: https://github.com/sion-rgb/tactical-slash/tree/main/assets/characters/player
- Original nature meshes: Kenney Nature Kit, CC0 1.0: https://kenney.nl/assets/nature-kit
- Optimized rock and bush GLB copies: https://github.com/sion-rgb/tactical-slash/tree/main/assets/environment
- Derivation and license audit: https://github.com/sion-rgb/tactical-slash/blob/main/THIRD_PARTY_ASSETS.md

These third-party meshes were not created by this project's author. The game's custom movement, missions, camera, environment layout, user interface and effects are original project code. Three.js and Vite retain their respective open-source licenses.


## High-fidelity art additions (2026-10-31)
- **Grab3D Man Base Mesh Rigged, CC0**: https://grab3d.com/models/characters/man-base-character/ — the real-proportions mesh, textures, skeleton and walk/run clips underpin the player character. Our custom thobe, shemagh, agal and sleeves are procedural additions in `saudi-hero.js`.
- **Innerscene Palm, Detailed, CC0**: https://www.innerscene.com/tools/library/3d-parts/palm-detailed-a8777b8c — the 8.9 m tall detailed palm tree; submeshes are merged into a shared mesh and cloned across the village.
- **ambientCG Plaster001 and PavingStones121, CC0**: https://ambientcg.com/view?id=Plaster001 and https://ambientcg.com/view?id=PavingStones121 — scanned plaster and paving surfaces. Albedo/normal maps are derived/converted to small WebP files; courtyard color is art-directed to sand tones.
